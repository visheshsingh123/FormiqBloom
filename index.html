07 / Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few things you might be wondering about before taking the next step.

Have something else in mind?
What information is needed to start?

A brief description of your business, your goals, the services you need, and any existing website or brand materials is a useful start. We’ll clarify content, features, budget, and responsibilities during discovery.

How is the delivery timeline decided?

The timeline depends on the agreed scope, content readiness, integrations, and review availability. We’ll agree on milestones before work begins and discuss changes if the scope or dependencies shift.

Can you update an existing website?

Yes, subject to reviewing its platform, code, access, and condition. That review helps decide whether targeted improvements or a rebuild is the more practical approach.

Who owns the domain?

Your domain should be registered in your name or your business’s name, in an account you control. We can agree on help with DNS and setup without transferring ownership to FormiqBloom.

What does ongoing maintenance include?

It depends on your support plan. We’ll specify the monitoring, updates, backups if included, support arrangements, and any content changes covered. New features or work outside that scope are quoted separately.

Can the AI assistant answer questions about my business?

It can be configured around business information you approve, such as services, policies, and FAQs. We’ll agree on boundaries and how it directs visitors to human help. AI can make mistakes, so important answers should be checked and maintained.

What happens if AI usage exceeds the agreed allowance?

We agree on usage allowances, monitoring, and overage handling before launch. The plan will specify whether usage is capped, paused, or continued at an agreed additional cost, and how you’re notified.
